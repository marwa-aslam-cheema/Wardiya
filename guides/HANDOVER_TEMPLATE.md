## 3. Daily use

**Employees:** open the link → sign in with code + PIN → tap **Check in** on arrival and **Check out** when leaving. Allow location when the phone asks.

**Managers (admin):**
- **Dashboard:** who is in, late or missing today.
- **Attendance log:** filter by date, employee, branch or status. Fix a forgotten check-out with **Edit**. A reason is required and recorded.
- **Monthly report:** at month end, choose the month → **Export CSV** → open in Excel → send to payroll.
- **Employees:** add new joiners, reset forgotten PINs (**Edit → Starting PIN**), and **Deactivate** leavers (their history is kept).

## 4. Your data

- All records are in your Google Sheet. Do **not** rename the tabs or the header row.
- Back up any time: **File → Download → Microsoft Excel**.
- PINs are stored as secure hashes (the hidden columns). Nobody, including us, can read them.
- Every correction is listed in **Audit log** and in the `audit` tab.

## 5. Good practice

- Keep at least **two admin accounts**.
- Start in **Flag** mode for the first week, then switch to **Block** if you want strict on-site check-in.
- Once a year (January): **Wardiya menu → Archive last year's records** to keep the app fast.

## 6. Limits to know

The system runs on Google's free Apps Script service, which is designed for teams of up to about 100 staff. Google sets daily usage quotas and can change them. If a quota is ever reached, the app shows an error until the quota resets (within 24 hours). Data is never lost.

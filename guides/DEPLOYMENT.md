# Publish your landing page and live demo (free, about 20 minutes)

This gives you a public link like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/wardiya/` to put in every sales message.

You need: a computer, a free GitHub account and a free Gmail address. No credit card.

---

## Step 1 — Create a business Gmail (5 min)

1. Go to <https://accounts.google.com/signup>.
2. Pick a professional address, for example `wardiya.software@gmail.com` or `wardiya.attendance@gmail.com`. Try a few if the first is taken.
3. Use this address for GitHub, outreach and the landing page.

## Step 2 — Put your contact details in the landing page (3 min)

Open `docs/index.html` in any text editor (Notepad works). Use *Find* to replace:

| Find | Replace with |
|---|---|
| `YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com` | your business Gmail |
| `YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER` | your WhatsApp number in international format, digits only, e.g. `2189XXXXXXXX` |

Save the file.

## Step 3 — Create a GitHub account (3 min)

1. Go to <https://github.com/signup> and sign up with the business Gmail.
2. Choose a short username. It becomes part of your link (`username.github.io`).
3. Verify your email.

## Step 4 — Upload the project (5 min)

1. On GitHub, click **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
2. Repository name: `wardiya`. Visibility: **Public** (free Pages needs public). Tick **Add a README file**. Click **Create repository**.
3. In the new repository click **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Unzip `wardiya.zip` on your computer. Open the `wardiya` folder, select **everything inside it** (`docs`, `backend`, `src`, `guides`, `test`, `README.md`, `build.js`) and drag it onto the GitHub page.
5. Wait for the upload, then click **Commit changes**.

> Check: the repository's top level should show the folders `docs`, `backend`, `src`… If you see one folder called `wardiya` instead, you uploaded the outer folder. Delete it and upload the *contents*.

## Step 5 — Turn on GitHub Pages (2 min)

1. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages** (left menu).
2. Under **Build and deployment → Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Branch: **main**, folder: **/docs**. Click **Save**.
4. Wait 1–3 minutes and refresh. GitHub shows *"Your site is live at https://USERNAME.github.io/wardiya/"*.

## Step 6 — Test it (2 min)

- Open `https://USERNAME.github.io/wardiya/`. The landing page should load.
- Click **Live demo** → **Try as Employee** → **Check in**. Your phone asks for location permission; allow it.
- Open the same link on your phone too. That is what clients will see.

**Your public links:**
- Landing page: `https://USERNAME.github.io/wardiya/`
- Demo: `https://USERNAME.github.io/wardiya/app/`

## Updating later

Edit a file on GitHub (open it → pencil icon → **Commit changes**). The site updates within about a minute.

## Optional: your own domain (costs money, only with your approval)

A domain like `wardiya.app` costs roughly $10–20/year. It isn't needed to get your first client. Add it later from **Settings → Pages → Custom domain**.

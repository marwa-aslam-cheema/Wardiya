Questions

Where is our data stored?

In a Google Sheet inside your own Google account. You can open it, back it up or export it any time. We don't keep a copy on our servers.

Do employees need to install an app?

No. They open a link in their phone browser, and they can add it to their home screen to use like an app. It works on iPhone and Android.

Can staff check in from home?

You decide. With geofencing on, check-ins outside the branch radius are either flagged for you to review or blocked completely.

Is it secure?

Each employee has a personal PIN, which is stored as a salted hash, never as plain text. Accounts lock after repeated wrong PINs. Admin pages are protected on the server. Every correction is recorded in an audit log with a reason.

Are there usage limits?

It runs on Google's free Apps Script service. It comfortably handles teams of up to about 100 staff. Older years can be archived with one click to keep it fast. For larger teams we'll tell you honestly whether a different setup suits you better.

Does it calculate salaries?

It produces the attendance figures payroll needs: days, hours, late minutes, overtime and leave. On Premium we format the export to match your payroll or WPS provider's template. The salary payment itself stays with your bank or payroll provider.

What happens after the support period?

The system keeps working, because it's yours. The optional care plan covers changes and support. Without it, we can quote for one-off changes.
